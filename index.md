## Biography
I studied physics for my bachelor's degree at the Ruprecht-Karls-University Heidelberg, where I also obtained my master's degree in physics. Alongside this study, I also completed a bachelor's degree in psychology at the Ruprecht-Karls-University Heidelberg. Afterwards, I moved to Tübingen, where I was a PhD Candidate in psychology at the Leibniz Insitut für Wissensmedien (IWM). Currently, I am completing my PhD at the Department of Psychology at the University of Tübingen in the Applied Cognitive Psychology research group of Prof. Huff.

## Research Interests

Decision making and belief updating under uncertainty, metacognition, narrative comprehension, video perception, mind wandering.
